Billing and Account Policy

SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT. Invented figures for a portfolio demo. Not the policy of any real provider and not to be presented as such.

Billing cycle

Accounts bill monthly in advance. The statement is generated on the same calendar day each month and payment is due 21 days after generation. A cycle cannot be moved more than once in a 12 month period.

Late payment

A late fee of $9.00 applies when a balance remains unpaid 5 days past the due date. Service is suspended for non-payment at 45 days past due. A suspended account is restored within 60 minutes of the past-due balance being cleared. A reconnection fee of $25.00 applies to accounts restored after suspension.

Autopay and paperless

Enrolling in autopay with a linked bank account reduces the monthly bill by $5.00. Enrolling with a credit card reduces it by $2.50. Paperless billing is required for both discounts. The discount appears on the first full cycle after enrollment, not immediately.

Promotional pricing

Introductory rates run for 12 or 24 months as stated at signup. When a promotion expires the account moves to standard rate on the next cycle. Customers are notified on the statement one cycle before expiry. Expired promotions cannot be reinstated, but the account can be moved to any currently advertised offer the customer qualifies for.

Equipment fees

A modem rental is $14.00 per month. A customer-owned modem from the approved device list removes this fee entirely. Each additional TV box is $8.50 per month. The first box is included with any TV package.

Equipment return

Returned equipment must arrive within 30 days of service cancellation. Unreturned equipment is charged at $120.00 for a modem and $90.00 for a TV box. Returns are accepted at any retail location or by prepaid shipping label. A receipt is issued at drop-off and should be kept until the charge clears the account.

Service credits

A credit for a confirmed outage is applied automatically at one day of service value per full day of outage, provided the outage exceeded 24 continuous hours. Credits for outages under 24 hours are issued only on request. Representatives may issue a goodwill credit up to $25.00 without approval.

Moving service

A transfer to a new address within the service area carries no installation fee if requested at least 14 days before the move date. Requests inside 14 days carry a $49.00 expedite fee. Promotional pricing transfers with the account provided the plan is available at the new address.

Internet and TV Troubleshooting

SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT. Written for a portfolio demo. Not the policy of any real provider and not to be presented as such.

No internet connection at all

  1. Confirm the modem has power and the power light is solid, not blinking.
  2. Unplug the modem and router from power. Wait 60 seconds. Plug the modem back in first and wait for the online light to go solid, which can take up to 5 minutes. Then plug the router back in.
  3. Check that the coaxial or fiber cable is finger-tight at both the wall and the modem.
  4. Connect a laptop directly to the modem with an ethernet cable. If that works, the problem is the router or Wi-Fi, not the line.
  5. If the online light never goes solid after two reboot attempts, the line needs a technician.

Slow speeds

  1. Run a speed test on a device connected by ethernet, not Wi-Fi. Wi-Fi results are not a measure of the line.
  2. Compare the result to the plan speed. Anything above 80 percent of plan speed on ethernet is considered normal.
  3. If ethernet speeds are normal but Wi-Fi is slow, the issue is coverage. Move the router off the floor, away from metal and away from microwaves, and switch devices to the 5 GHz band where available.
  4. If ethernet speeds are below 50 percent of plan speed across two tests an hour apart, escalate for a line check.

Intermittent drops

  1. Note whether drops correlate with weather, time of day, or heavy usage.
  2. Check for loose or damaged coaxial cable, especially splitters added by the customer. Remove any customer-installed splitter and test again.
  3. Drops that happen at the same time each day usually indicate a node congestion issue, which requires a network ticket rather than a home visit.

TV box shows no signal

  1. Confirm the TV input is set to the correct HDMI port.
  2. Restart the box by unplugging it for 30 seconds.
  3. If a single channel is missing but others work, it is a programming issue, not an equipment issue.
  4. If all channels are missing and internet works, the box likely needs a refresh signal sent from the account side.

When to send a technician

Send a technician when the modem will not come online after two reboots, when ethernet speeds are under half of plan speed across repeated tests, when there is visible damage to the line at the property, or when the customer has already had the same issue recur within 14 days of a previous fix.

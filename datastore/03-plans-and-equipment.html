Internet plans

  • 300 Mbps — $40.00/month. Suited to 1 to 3 devices, streaming in HD.
  • 500 Mbps — $60.00/month. Suited to 4 to 8 devices, 4K streaming, work from home.
  • 1 Gig — $80.00/month. Suited to large households, heavy uploads, multiple 4K streams.
  • 5 Gig — $180.00/month. Fiber service areas only. Requires a compatible router.

All plans include unlimited data. There are no overage charges on any residential plan.

TV packages

  • TV Core — $30.00/month with any internet plan. Approximately 75 channels.
  • TV Select — $65.00/month. Approximately 175 channels including regional sports.
  • TV Premier — $95.00/month. Approximately 300 channels including premium movie networks.

Mobile

Mobile lines are available only to customers with an active internet plan. The first line is $15.00/month with 5 GB of high-speed data, and additional lines are $15.00 each up to four lines. Unlimited data lines are $45.00/month.

Approved customer-owned modems

Any DOCSIS 3.1 modem is supported on plans up to 1 Gig. Fiber service areas require the provider-supplied optical network terminal, which cannot be customer-owned. Plans at 5 Gig require a multi-gig capable router with a 2.5 Gbps or faster LAN port.

Installation

Self-installation kits ship within 2 business days at no charge where the address has existing wiring. Professional installation is $99.00 and is required for new fiber drops, for addresses with no prior service, and for any install requiring new interior wiring.

Wi-Fi extenders

Extenders are $5.00 per month each. A typical single-floor home under 1,500 square feet does not need one. Homes over 2,500 square feet or with more than two floors usually need at least one. Extenders must be placed within range of the main router, roughly halfway between the router and the weak area, not in the weak area itself.
